About the Author

Hadiza Bagudu

A talented author holding a degree in International Studies from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, and a Microsoft Certified Professional. Residing in Abuja with her husband and two children, she authored the captivating novel "Fantah" and this heartfelt collection of poems.

Her poetry explores the depths of emotion and the beauty of existence, navigating love, loss, and life's subtle moments with lyrical grace and insight.
